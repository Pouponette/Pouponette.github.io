Hermy Butler

Hermy Butler is a private, personal household assistant run by Jerome Philippe on his own computer in South Australia. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

It uses Google sign-in so that the household's own assistant agents can help manage the family's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts: reading and sorting email, drafting and sending replies, and adding reminders and events.

Only the Google accounts belonging to the household use this app.

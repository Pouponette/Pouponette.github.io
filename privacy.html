Hermy Butler – Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

Hermy Butler is a private household assistant. It is used only by Jerome Philippe and members of his household, with their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts (read-only) for that owner's own account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the account owner asks for, such as summarising and sorting email, drafting and sending replies, and managing calendar events and documents.

To do this, the household's assistant software sends the relevant content to the AI model provider it is configured to use (currently Anthropic's Claude), or to an AI model running locally on the household computer.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the household's own computer. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the AI model provider as described above.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Any account owner can remove this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
